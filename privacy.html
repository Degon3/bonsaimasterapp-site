Privacy Policy

Bonsai Master is designed with privacy in mind.
Your data stays on your device.

Local-first by design

Bonsai Master stores your data locally on your device.

This includes:

  • Bonsai leaves
  • Stress score
  • Daily goal
  • Selected draining apps
  • Flexible days
  • Focus progress
  • App settings

No cloud storage is used.

App usage access

If you grant app usage access, Bonsai Master reads the total time spent in the apps you select.

The app does not read:

  • App content
  • Messages
  • Screens
  • Keystrokes

Notifications

Notifications are used only for reminders you explicitly enable, such as:

  • Focus reminders
  • Pruning reminders
  • Daily goal reminders

You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

No data selling or sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, or sent to third parties.

Bonsai Master:

  • Does not run ads
  • Does not use trackers

Data retention and deletion

Bonsai Master stores app data locally on your device only. This data is retained on your device until you choose to delete it, reset the app, clear the app data from your device settings, or uninstall the app.

You can delete your local app data at any time from within the app by going to: Settings → Data & permissions → Start over.

When you use this option, the app deletes the local data stored by Bonsai Master on your device. Because Bonsai Master does not use accounts, cloud storage, external servers, analytics services, advertising SDKs, or trackers, we do not store a copy of your app data on our servers.

If you uninstall Bonsai Master or clear the app data from your Android device settings, the local data stored by the app is also removed from your device.

Reset and control

You can erase all local data at any time using:

Settings → Data & permissions → Start over
